About

WHAT IS
GROWTH ROOM?

Growth Room is a structured, cohort-based monthly personal development program designed exclusively for Muslim youth aged 18–30. Each cohort runs for 3 months and includes live online sessions, weekly challenges, and a close-knit community of growth-minded peers.

Unlike generic self-help programs, Growth Room is built entirely on Islamic principles — drawing from the wisdom of the Quran, the Sunnah, and the rich tradition of Islamic scholars — while incorporating the best of modern psychology, neuroscience, and productivity science.
