Growth Room

A structured monthly personal development programme for Muslim youth, grounded in Islamic principles and modern personal growth science.

About

What Is Growth Room?

Growth Room is a structured, cohort-based monthly personal development program designed exclusively for Muslim youth aged 18–30. Each cohort runs for 3 months and includes live online sessions, weekly challenges, and a close-knit community of growth-minded peers.

Unlike generic self-help programmes, Growth Room is built on Islamic principles, drawing from the Quran, the Sunnah, and the rich tradition of Islamic scholars, while pulling in the best of modern psychology, neuroscience, and productivity science.

Outcomes

What You'll Gain

Clarity of Purpose

Discover your unique purpose aligned with your faith, talents, and the needs of your community.

Islamic Self-Discipline

Build Mujahada (spiritual struggle) and Tawakkul (reliance on Allah) as the foundation of genuine discipline.

Emotional Intelligence

Understand and regulate your emotions through the lens of Islamic wisdom and modern psychology.

Productivity & Focus

Apply proven Islamic time-management and focus principles to become more productive in your deen and dunya.

Spiritual Connection

Deepen your relationship with Allah through structured practices in Dhikr, Quran, and daily worship habits.

Community of Growth

Connect with a curated community of driven Muslim youth who push each other toward their best selves.

Process

How It Works

1

Apply

Submit your application with a brief note on your goals and why Growth Room is the right fit for you.

2

Get Accepted

Our team reviews each application to ensure cohort quality. Accepted members receive a welcome pack and cohort schedule.

3

Join Monthly Sessions

Attend 4 live sessions per month with Soban, complete weekly challenges, and engage with your cohort community.

4

Transform

Emerge from the program with clarity, discipline, spiritual depth, and a network of lifelong brothers and sisters in growth.
