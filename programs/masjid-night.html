Masjid Night

A 3-4 hour evening with Soban Attari inside a modern mosque — sessions, practical faraiz training, indoor activities, and a barbecue dinner hosted by Soban Attari.

Overview

What Is Masjid Night?

A relaxed, unhurried evening where attendees spend real time with Soban Attari — not just a lecture and an exit, but a night built around conversation, training, activities, and a meal together.

01

Interesting Sessions

Engaging talks and open conversation with Soban Attari, built around real questions.

02

Practical Faraiz Training

A hands-on walkthrough of how to actually perform the daily faraiz — the step-by-step method of Namaz, wudu, and more, practised together.

03

Indoor Activities

Lighthearted activities that bring the community together.

04

Barbecue Dinner

A relaxed barbecue dinner hosted by Soban Attari for everyone attending.

05

3-4 Hours, One Evening

A complete evening at a modern mosque — session, training, activities, and dinner, all in one sitting.

Soban Attari is a Pakistani speaker who works with the youth on faith, family, and modern life through honest, plain-spoken talks.
