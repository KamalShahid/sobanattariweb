About

ABOUT THE
SEMINAR

The Youth Seminar is Soban Attari's flagship institutional programme: a high-energy, deeply impactful event designed for universities, schools, corporate organisations, and community institutions.

Whether delivered as a half-day intensive or a full-day immersive experience, the Youth Seminar combines compelling lectures, interactive Q&A, real-life case studies, and personal reflection exercises to create lasting impact.

Hundreds of institutions across Pakistan have hosted this seminar, from premier universities to community mosques, with consistently transformative results.

✓

Tailored to your audience, whether university students, professionals, or community members.

✓

Available in Urdu, English, or a bilingual format depending on your institution's needs

✓

Post-event resources, recordings, and follow-up engagement materials provided

✓

Suitable for audiences of 50 to 5,000 plus, in person or via live-streaming.

Curriculum

TOPICS
COVERED

01
Identity & Purpose

Who am I? Why am I here? Islamic clarity on identity, purpose, and the unique mission of every believer in this life.

02
Islam & Technology

How should a Muslim navigate social media, AI, gaming, entertainment, and the digital world with Islamic principles?

03
Mental Health

Understanding anxiety, depression, and stress through an Islamic lens, and finding healing through faith, community, and practical tools.

04
Relationships & Marriage

Navigating friendships, family dynamics, and the path to marriage, with Islamic wisdom on love, boundaries, and commitment.

05
Career & Ambition

How to pursue worldly ambition without losing your spiritual grounding: halal income, meaningful work, and Islamic success.

06
Science & Faith

Why science and Islam are not opponents but allies, exploring the Quran's invitation to scientific inquiry and the miracle of creation.
