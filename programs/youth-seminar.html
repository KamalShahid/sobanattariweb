About

ABOUT THE
SEMINAR

The Youth Seminar is Soban Attari's flagship institutional program — a powerful, high-energy, deeply impactful event designed for universities, schools, corporate organizations, and community institutions.

Whether delivered as a half-day intensive or a full-day immersive experience, the Youth Seminar combines compelling lectures, interactive Q&A, real-life case studies, and personal reflection exercises to create lasting impact.

Hundreds of institutions across Pakistan have hosted this seminar — from premier universities to community mosques — with consistently transformative results.
