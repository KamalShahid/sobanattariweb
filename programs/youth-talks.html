Overview

WHAT ARE
YOUTH TALKS?

Youth Talks are live, interactive sessions hosted by Soban Attari specifically designed for Muslim youth aged 15–30. Unlike traditional lectures, Youth Talks are conversational and question-driven — where no topic is off the table.

From questions about faith and doubt, to science, relationships, career, and mental health — Youth Talks tackle the real struggles of a generation navigating modernity with an Islamic compass.

Feature 01
Real Questions Welcome

No question is too controversial, too basic, or too bold. Youth Talks are a space of radical honesty and genuine Islamic guidance.

Feature 02
Islamic Clarity

Every answer is rooted in Quran, Sunnah, and established scholarly consensus — but delivered in clear, contemporary language.

Feature 03
Modern Context

Topics are always current and relevant — social media, AI, mental health, career, identity, relationships, and more.

The Experience

WHAT TO
EXPECT

Interactive Q&A Format

Every session is structured around live questions from the audience — submitted digitally or asked in person. Soban responds thoughtfully and directly.

Science & Faith Topics

Topics spanning the relationship between modern science and Islamic belief — evolution, cosmology, psychology, neuroscience, and more.

Practical Life Guidance

Actionable Islamic guidance on real-life decisions: career choices, relationships, mental wellness, family dynamics, and daily spiritual practice.
