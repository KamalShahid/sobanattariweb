FOS

Friends of Soban Attari — students and professionals working together inside their own institutes and universities to learn, grow, and serve.

Overview

What Is FOS?

FOS brings together students and professionals who want to keep growing, inside their own institutes and universities, working closely alongside Soban Attari.

01

Institute Chapters

FOS groups are organized inside universities and institutes, led by the members themselves.

02

Regular Lectures

Soban Attari delivers regular sessions at members' homes, campuses, and institutes.

03

Growing Together

Members support each other's learning, habits, and daily growth.

04

Bringing Youth to Events

FOS members help organize and bring more youth to Soban Attari's talks and seminars.
