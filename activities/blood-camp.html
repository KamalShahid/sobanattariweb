The Mission

SAVING LIVES
TOGETHER

Inspired by the Islamic principle that saving one life is like saving all of humanity, Soban Attari organizes regular blood donation camps across Pakistan. These camps bring together volunteers, medical teams, and communities in a powerful act of collective compassion.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said: "The most beloved people to Allah are those who are most beneficial to the people." Each blood donation camp is a direct embodiment of this principle, turning faith into action that saves real lives.

Quranic Foundation
"Whoever saves one life, it is as if he has saved all of mankind."
Quran 5:32
