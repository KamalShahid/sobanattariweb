Meet the Scholar

Meet Soban Attari

Islamic Scholar Public Speaker Educator Author Mentor Life Coach

Soban Attari is a Pakistani motivational speaker, Islamic scholars, and social media influencer. He holds CA inter and MBA degrees and has become one of the best-known and most positive motivational speakers in Pakistan.

Soban Attari regularly conducts workshops and delivers lectures on a variety of topics, including Islamic teachings, personal development, and leadership, in almost all recognized and well-known institutes across the country. His TV program “Islam and Science” is also highly informative and available on his YouTube channel, where he has a large and dedicated fan following.

With his laser-sharp logic, crystal-clear explanation, and easy-to-understand talks, Soban Attari has become a source of inspiration and motivation among the youth.
