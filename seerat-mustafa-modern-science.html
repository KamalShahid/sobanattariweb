About the Book

The Sunnah, Studied Closely

Seerat e Mustafa & Modern Science is Soban Attari's study of the Prophetic life ﷺ read side by side with contemporary research. The first part takes the small, daily sunnahs — the midday rest (qailulah), the Prophet's ﷺ way of sleeping, the greeting of salaam, the smile, fragrance, the miswak and the giving of gifts — and sets each one against what sleep science, psychology, dentistry and the study of human connection have since found.

The second part turns to food and drink: eating while seated, eating with the hands, sharing a single dish, sipping water, and the medical reasoning behind them. The final part opens out to the Seerah as a complete code of life — the model welfare state of Madina, the Prophetic principles of leadership, guidance for professional life, and how the Seerah speaks to questions raised by artificial intelligence.

The book is written in Urdu.
