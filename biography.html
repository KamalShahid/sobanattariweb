Soban Attari Biography

A VOICE FOR
MODERN FAITH

Soban Attari is a renowned Islamic and motivational speaker recognized for his impactful speeches and engagements in various settings, including live events, conferences, and educational institutions.

With a strong social media presence and a dedicated following across various platforms, Soban Attari has effectively reached and inspired a wide audience. Soban Attari has actively worked with prestigious institutes and organizations at all levels, making a significant impact in the field of Islamic and motivational speaking. Born on 22nd September 1979, in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan, Soban Attari, at the age of 20 continued to be a dynamic force in transforming lives through his powerful speeches and dedicated efforts.

He hosted and participated in many TV programs. Among these, "Islam and Science" got much popularity. All of its episodes are available on his official YouTube channel.

His podcast, web series and short clips on a wide variety of topics are a source of inspiration for many (all are easily available on his official social media platforms).

Qualification

EDUCATION
JOURNEY

Soban Attari has done his intermediate from Government College University (GCU), Lahore and B.Com from University of the Punjab, Lahore. Soban Attari's education journey also includes CA Inter and MBA Executive. Along with his regular studies, Soban Attari actively participated in co-curricular activities and sports in school. Don Bosco High School, an old and spacious educational institute located in the heart of the city, is famous for its basketball team and regular academic activities.

Life Achievements

ACADEMIC
EXCELLENCE

Soban Attari achieved remarkable success during his academic journey, winning numerous declamations and speech contests in school and consistently excelling in exams. Following his school years, Soban Attari pursued higher education at Government College University Lahore, a prestigious and renowned institution in Pakistan known for its contributions to self-knowledge, employment, quality training, research, and development. The esteemed alumni of this institute hold prominent positions across various fields. Soban Attari's time at Government College University played a pivotal role in shaping his talents and skills.

During his college life, Soban Attari showcased his dominance in academics, sports, and other activities. He fondly recalls his favorite spots on the campus, the oval ground, and the library. It was in the library that he cultivated a habit of book reading, which continues to this day, with Soban Attari considering books as his best friends. After completing his education at Government College University, Soban Attari pursued commerce studies from Hailey College of Commerce at University of the Punjab. The institute boasted qualified teachers and provided an enriching university ambience, further unlocking Soban Attari's inner potential and contributing to the development of his strong personality, built on the foundation laid by Government College University.

Family Background

ROOTS THAT
SHAPED HIM

Shahan Murshad, Soban Attari's father, was a businessman and took a keen interest in the upbringing of their children, including Soban Attari. His family has a rich history, with his grandfather, Mr. Younis, being an emigrant who worked in Bata Shoes Company. Soban Attari's family played an important role in his life and upbringing.

A Life-Changing Turn

CA, DAWATEISLAMI
& A NEW JOURNEY

Next phase of life was Charted Accountancy (CA), a rare discipline of education at that time when every student was yearning to become doctor and engineer. In the 2nd year of CA, Soban's life took a U-turn. An event that brought immense changes in his life. Introduction and joining Dawateislami, an international non-political, non-profit Islamic organization, which is propagating Islamic knowledge and enlightening the message of Islam across the globe. Soban Attari became disciple of Hazrat Maulana Ilyas Qadri Sahib, the founder of Dawateislami and an internationally acclaimed scholar of Islam, writer and Sufi (saint). Dominant feature of his personality which impressed Soban Attari was his actions, not only words, but practical approach towards Islam.

Hazrat Maulana Ilyas Qadri Sahib's message is peace and his style is so unique and appealing that Dawateislami is progressing rapidly. Like millions, lifestyle and mindset of Soban Attari was also changed. Soban was later assigned the education department of Dawateislami, as provincial head. His duty was to influence youth, especially students and teachers and propagate the invitation of goodness among them.

So a new journey started. Soban Attari worked day and night to establish this new wing of Dawateislami. The primary focus was Universities. To gather all the scattered resources and establish a new system, resource mobilization was a daunting challenge at that time. Soban's talent of public speaking and leadership were the key ingredients in clearing the obstacles and way towards progress. Affectionate patronage and supervision of his spiritual mentor, Ilyas Qadri sahib, worked wonders. Goals were met. People were shocked to see the results.

Work Approach

ISLAM &
SCIENCE

His topic, especially scientific approach towards Islam, Islam and Science became his brand. His speech encompasses wide ranging topics from basic Islamic teachings to general motivation, social ills and their solution, ethics and moral values.

Furthermore, his Podcasts, programs and short clips on goal setting, leadership, problem solving and other related topics of personality development are widely liked and source of inspiration for people belonging to all walks of life. After these accomplishments, next target was medical doctors and hospitals. One of the busiest and most important professional of our society.

With his old connections, charismatic personality and laser sharp logics, Soban got their attention, built a team and started visiting the institutes for the noble cause. Many joined and this caravan started progressing day-by-day. That initial working with devotion and loyalty bore fruit. Doctors started celebrating the Meelad of Holy Prophet (peace be upon him), travelling in Madani Qafla, left malpractices, doing short courses and became practicing Muslims.

Reach of Activities

Soban Attari, known for his noble cause, has extensively traveled across Pakistan, making a significant impact. One noteworthy aspect of Soban's personality is his dedication to welfare and philanthropy. During the challenging days of the first wave of the COVID-19 pandemic, when the world was grappling with severe health and financial crises, Ilyas Qadri Sahib initiated a nationwide blood collection drive. Soban Attari was entrusted with the responsibility of overseeing the drive in the Punjab region. In addition to various projects aimed at uplifting the general population, Ilyas Qadri Sahib's focus extended to one of the most vulnerable segments of society, the thalassemia patients, for whom fresh blood transfusions are essential for survival. A massive blood collection drive was initiated under this initiative, spearheaded by Soban Attari as part of his impactful biography.

Soban Attari started the project in Punjab with devoted workers, observing all the recommended protective measures. Credible organizations like Shoukat Khanum Memorial Hospital, Sundas Foundation etc. became partners. Initial assessment was done, areas were selected, and Soban Attari travelled across Punjab, started a proper media campaign and gave talks to channels. He Interviewed prominent medical experts and started the project with full zeal and zest. He used to visit the camps before and after the blood collection procedure for performance evaluation and feedback. At that time, Dawateislami collected more than 55,000+ bags of blood. Soban Attari's share was 10,000+ blood bags. During the campaign, Soban Attari visited several thalassemia centers, interviewed patients and parents and recorded programs for public awareness.

Another notable activity is plantation. Climate change is the fact of life and it's happening right now. Global warming is eroding the entire fabric of Earth and one of the most important steps to tackle the situation is plantation. Green is the game changer. FGRF welfare division of Dawateislami started plantation drive and Soban Attari was, once more, given a core responsibility. He recorded many programs and short clips on the importance of plantation, all these were broadcasted on Madani Channel and other mainstream social media. He visited many educational institutes, government and private offices and done plantation with VC's, HOD's, Principals, high grade officers and delivered lectures among students for proper awareness about one of the most important activities.

When the team of professional doctors was well built, Soban Attari managed free eye testing, blood screening camps for the students and teachers of madrasah and Jamia. Thousands were given free medical counseling, medicines and glasses. This philanthropy activity spread across Pakistan. Beneficiaries were mostly those who belong to lower middle-class category of society.

In 2022, heavy rainfall and floods have submerged almost one-third of Pakistan. In that crisis of unimaginable proportion, Soban Attari joined relief and recovery campaign for flood affected people with FGRF. His special focus was donations and medical camps as health and hygiene were major concerns in those areas. Soban visited Fazilpur and other south Punjab regions which were affected along with doctors. Medicines and other essential items were distributed among the affected people.

Soban Attari supervised many welfare projects of FGRF (Faizan Global Relief Foundation), especially blood collection for thalassemia patients, plantation and flood relief activities. He managed several blood donation camps across Pakistan and collected 10,000+ bags of blood. He has done plantations in many educational institutions, private and public offices along with notable dignitaries. He also delivers lectures among students and runs a social media campaign to create awareness among the masses. He organized relief and recovery activities especially medical camps in flood affected areas.
