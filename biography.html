Who He Is

A VOICE FOR
MODERN FAITH

Soban Attari is an Islamic scholar and motivational speaker from Lahore. He spends most of the year travelling to universities, mosques, and corporate halls to sit down with students and professionals and talk through faith, family, and modern life in plain language.

Online, his lectures and short clips reach millions every week. Offline, he is the same person on stage as he is in a one-to-one room: direct, curious, and unafraid of the harder questions.

He was born in 1979 in Lahore, Pakistan, and still calls the city home.

His Journey

FROM STUDENT
TO SHEPHERD

A life shaped by faith, study, and a habit of showing up for the work, from a final commerce degree at Punjab University to stages in thousands of institutions across Pakistan.

1979
Born in Lahore

Soban Attari was born in Lahore, Punjab, in September 1979, into a household where faith and discipline shaped daily life.

Uni
Punjab University, Hailey College of Commerce

He read for his B.Com at Hailey College of Commerce, Punjab University in Lahore, and went on to complete CA Inter and an MBA Executive. The final degree gave him the analytical training that still shapes how he reasons through Islamic and modern questions in public.

CA
A U-Turn That Changed Everything

In the second year of his CA studies, Soban encountered Dawateislami, an international non-political Islamic organisation. He became a disciple of Maulana Ilyas Qadri, scholar, writer and founder of Dawateislami. What stayed with him most was Maulana Qadri's practical approach: not just words, but action.

DI
Provincial Head, Education Department

As Provincial Head of the Dawateislami Education Department, Soban was asked to focus on students and teachers and to build the wing's reach inside universities. The work moved fast, and the results turned heads.

TV
Islam & Science on Madani Channel

When Madani Channel went on air, Soban joined the core management and managed the Punjab region. He designed and hosted "Islam and Science", which became one of the channel's top-rated programmes. He also hosted several others, all of which now sit on his YouTube channel.

His Approach

ISLAM &
SCIENCE

His signature topic, a scientific approach towards Islam, became his brand. His speeches stretch from basic Islamic teachings to motivation, social problems and their solutions, ethics, and moral values.

His podcasts, web series "Design Your Life," and short clips on goal setting, leadership, problem solving and personality development are widely liked and a source of inspiration for people belonging to all walks of life.

He also extended his work to medical professionals, building a team of doctors who began celebrating Meelad of the Holy Prophet ﷺ, travelling in Madani Qafla, and stepping away from malpractices.

Reach of Activities
Blood Donation Drive

During COVID-19, Soban was asked to oversee the blood collection drive across Punjab for thalassemia patients. Partnering with Shaukat Khanum Memorial Hospital, Sundas Foundation, and other credible organisations, he travelled across the province, ran a media campaign, interviewed medical experts, and visited camps to check on performance. Dawateislami collected more than 55,000 bags of blood, with Soban's personal share over 10,000.

Plantation Drive

Soban was given the core responsibility of FGRF's plantation initiative. He recorded programmes and short clips on the importance of planting trees, broadcast on Madani Channel and mainstream social media. He visited educational institutes, government and private offices, planting alongside VCs, HODs, principals, and senior officers, while raising awareness among students about climate change and Islam's call to care for the Earth.

Flood Relief, 2022

In 2022, when heavy rain and floods submerged almost one-third of Pakistan, Soban Attari joined FGRF's relief and recovery campaign. His focus was donations and medical camps, since health and hygiene were the urgent concerns. He visited Fazilpur and other parts of south Punjab with doctors, distributing medicines and essential items to affected families. He also managed free eye testing and blood screening camps for madrasah and Jamia students and teachers across Pakistan.
