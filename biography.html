His Journey

FROM STUDENT
TO SHEPHERD

A life shaped by faith, education, and an unrelenting desire to serve — from the halls of Government College University to the stages of thousands of institutions across Pakistan and beyond.

1979
Born in Lahore, Pakistan

Soban Attari was born on 22nd September, 1979, in Lahore, Punjab, Pakistan. His father, Shahan Murshad, was a businessman who took a keen interest in the upbringing of his children. His grandfather, Mr. Younis, was an emigrant who worked at Bata Shoes Company. From his earliest years, Soban was raised in a household where faith and discipline were central values.

Early
Don Bosco High School

Soban attended Don Bosco High School — an old and spacious educational institute in the heart of Lahore, famous for its basketball team and regular academic activities. During school, he actively participated in co-curricular activities and sports, winning numerous declamations and speech contests and consistently excelling in exams.

GCU
Government College University, Lahore

Soban pursued his Intermediate from Government College University (GCU), Lahore — a prestigious institution known for its contributions to self-knowledge, employment, quality training, research, and development. He dominated in academics, sports, and activities, spending time at his favourite spots: the oval ground and the library, where he cultivated a lifelong love of reading. "Books are my best friends," he has often said.

PU
Punjab University — B.Com & Higher Studies

After GCU, Soban pursued B.Com from Hailey College of Commerce at Punjab University, Lahore — further unlocking his inner potential. He later pursued CA Inter and MBA Executive, building a rare combination of spiritual depth and professional academic grounding.

CA
A U-Turn That Changed Everything

In the second year of his CA studies, Soban's life took a remarkable turn. He was introduced to Dawateislami — an international non-political, non-profit Islamic organization propagating Islamic knowledge across the globe. He became a disciple of Maulana Ilyas Qadri Sahib — an internationally acclaimed scholar of Islam, writer, Sufi saint and founder of Dawateislami. The dominant feature of Maulana Qadri's personality that impressed Soban was his practical approach: not merely words, but action.

DI
Provincial Head — Education Department, Dawateislami

Soban was assigned to the Education Department of Dawateislami as Provincial Head. His duty was to influence youth — especially students and teachers — and propagate the invitation of goodness among them. He worked day and night to establish this new wing, with a primary focus on universities. His talent for public speaking and leadership cleared the obstacles. Goals were met. People were shocked to see the results.

TV
Islam & Science — Madani Channel

When Madani Channel started its transmission, Soban was in the core management and was manager of the Punjab region. He was tasked with designing and hosting the program "Islam and Science" — which became one of the top-ranking programs of Madani Channel. All episodes are available on his official YouTube channel. He also hosted "Insaan Aik Herat Angez Takhleeq" (Human is a Marvelous Creation), "Dilchasp Batein," "Malumaat Hei Kuch Khaas," and appeared live on the morning show "Khule Ankh Sale ALLAH Kehte Kehte."

2023
Country Head — Professionals Department

As of 2023, Soban serves as Country Head of the Professionals Department of Dawateislami. His podcasts, web series "Design Your Life," and short clips on goal setting, leadership, and problem solving continue to inspire people from all walks of life. As he says: "Entire credit of my success goes to my parents. Their day and night sacrifices."

Reach of Activities
Blood Donation Drive

During COVID-19, Soban was entrusted with overseeing the blood collection drive across Punjab for thalassemia patients. Partnering with Shaukat Khanum Memorial Hospital, Sundas Foundation, and other credible organizations, he travelled across Punjab, conducted a proper media campaign, interviewed medical experts, and visited camps for performance evaluation. Dawateislami collected more than 55,000 bags of blood — Soban's personal share exceeds 10,000.

Plantation Drive

Soban was given the core responsibility of FGRF's plantation initiative. He recorded programs and short clips on the importance of plantation, broadcast on Madani Channel and mainstream social media. He visited educational institutes, government and private offices, conducting plantation with VCs, HODs, principals, and high-grade officers — raising awareness among students about climate change and Islam's call to care for the Earth.

Flood Relief — 2022

In 2022, when heavy rainfall and floods submerged almost one-third of Pakistan, Soban Attari joined FGRF's relief and recovery campaign. His special focus was donations and medical camps — as health and hygiene were major concerns. He visited Fazilpur and other south Punjab regions with doctors, distributing medicines and essential items to affected people. He also managed free eye testing and blood screening camps for madrasah and Jamia students and teachers across Pakistan.
